About Karen Carr

An art teacher and homeschool mom who kept using drawings to teach children wisely.

Karen loved drawing as a little girl, taught art in Indiana public schools, then used the same gifts of teaching, art, and curriculum-making while homeschooling her three boys.

The Wise Child materials grew out of that life. The purpose stayed plain: help children grow in wisdom by connecting God's truth to situations they can picture and remember.

For Parents

Warm and welcoming on the surface, serious about wisdom underneath.

That balance is the central design problem Karen handed over in her PDFs. The site should feel inviting enough for children and families, but strong enough to show that the books are teaching something real.

Karen's own direction stays the best summary: keep the drawings central, keep the words short, and help kids become wise.
