About Karen

Teacher first.
Illustrator too.

Karen loved drawing as a girl, taught art in Indiana public schools, and later homeschooled her three boys. She drew to help children understand ideas that words alone could make hard to see.

Now, as a grandmother, she is gathering those pictures and lessons so other families can use them. The medium is pictures. The purpose is wisdom.

How The Wise Child Book began

A blue chair, an open Bible, and a child asking for the picture again.

The first Wise Child pages grew out of ordinary family life. Karen read Proverbs, drew what she was learning, and explained the drawings to her young son. He kept coming back to hear them again.
