What is CarrToons for?

A warm place for families to open books that train wise habits.

CarrToons brings together explanation, rebuke, context, careful reading, and the daily situations where children learn wisdom.

Families can open a book, read a page, ask a question, and keep coming back.

Meet Karen

Why her story matters

These books grew out of decades of teaching, mothering, and drawing.

Karen loved drawing as a little girl, taught art in Indiana schools, homeschooled her three boys, taught in church, and kept reaching for pictures when words alone did not feel enough.

Families who open these books are receiving work that has already been tested in homes, classrooms, and church life.
